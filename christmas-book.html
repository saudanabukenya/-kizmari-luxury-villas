Is this a physical book?+

No. This is a digital book supplied as a downloadable PDF. The book and device mockups show how the digital edition can be presented. No printed book or device is included.

What exactly will I receive?+

One 63-page PDF, approximately 43 MB, with six design chapters, styling guidance, a family welcome and Kizmari contact links. The six chapters are Arrival, Gather, Celebrate, Retreat, Escape and Design Atelier.

How do I access my digital book?+

The purchase buttons take you to our Selar product page. Complete your purchase there and follow Selar’s access and download instructions to save your PDF. Use an email address you can access at checkout.

Can I read it on my phone or tablet?+

Yes, with an app that opens PDFs. The book uses landscape pages, so a tablet or computer offers a more spacious view. On a phone, turn the screen sideways and zoom in on the details.

What is the price?+

The digital book is US$14. Your final payable amount and any applicable taxes are shown by Selar at checkout. Other available currencies can be selected on the Selar product page.
