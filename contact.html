Before you ask

A few things

Is Kizmari open for stays?

Kizmari Luxury Villas is currently in development. Opening dates, rates, and booking details will be shared when they are ready. For opening enquiries, contact us using the email below.

Are these photographs of the finished villas?

The images shown here are AI-assisted design renderings. They communicate the intended look and feel, while construction and finishing work continue. Final spaces, furnishings, and finishes may differ.

What does the name Kizmari mean?

The name brings together Kizito and Mariam, the founder's children. That family connection is at the heart of the brand.

How do the villas and Kizmari Arts connect?

They are parts of the same family vision: spaces and creative pieces with their own character. Kizmari Arts shares paintings, handmade pots, and the work behind the pieces.
